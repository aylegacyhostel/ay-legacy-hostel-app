How to request deletion

To request deletion of personal information held by AY Legacy Hostel, email aylegacyhostel@gmail.com with the subject 'Personal Data Deletion Request'. You may also contact hostel management at 0546188278.

What to include

Provide your name and a way for us to identify your hostel record, such as your student/index number or the phone number used for registration. Do not send passwords, full identification document numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your request, verify that it comes from the correct person, review which records can be deleted, and explain any information that must be retained for legal, accounting, safety, or unresolved accommodation obligations. We will inform you when the request has been processed.

Stop WhatsApp messages

You can request that WhatsApp messages stop without requesting deletion of your hostel record. Email aylegacyhostel@gmail.com or contact management at 0546188278 and ask to withdraw WhatsApp messaging consent.

Third-party services

Some information may have been processed by service providers such as hosting, database, or messaging providers. Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to address deletion requests in those services subject to their retention rules and legal requirements.
