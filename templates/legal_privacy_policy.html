Who we are

AY Legacy Hostel, part of AY Legacy Group, provides student accommodation in Sunyani–Fiapre, behind Fiapre Community Park, Ghana. Contact us at aylegacyhostel@gmail.com or 0546188278.

Information we collect

When you enquire, apply, or reside at the hostel, we may collect your name, student/index number, phone and WhatsApp number, email, institution, program and year, room and bed assignment, payment and occupancy records, parent/guardian and emergency contact details, identification details, and information you choose to provide. Some records may include sensitive information; we limit access to staff who need it for hostel operations.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, administer accommodation, allocate rooms, maintain student and payment records, communicate safety and maintenance notices, resolve concerns, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations.

WhatsApp messages and consent

If you opt in, authorized hostel personnel may send hostel-related WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform, including notices about inspections, security, maintenance, payments, and accommodation. Messages are sent only to eligible recipients with recorded consent. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP where supported or contacting us at aylegacyhostel@gmail.com or 0546188278. Opting out of WhatsApp messages does not affect your accommodation or other necessary hostel communications.

Sharing and service providers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, database storage, and communications, including Meta/WhatsApp when you consent to WhatsApp messaging. We share only information needed to provide those services. We do not sell student personal information.

Security and retention

We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards to protect records. No internet system is completely secure. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for accommodation administration, disputes, accounting, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes, then delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw WhatsApp consent, by emailing aylegacyhostel@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify your identity. Some information may need to be retained to comply with law or resolve outstanding obligations.

Children and third-party contacts

The service is intended for hostel applicants and residents. If you provide details of a parent, guardian, or emergency contact, please inform them that their information is provided for hostel administration and emergencies.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version will be posted on this page.
